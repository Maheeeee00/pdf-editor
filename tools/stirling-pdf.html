Stirling PDF

A free, open-source toolbox for PDF files. Combine your notes, shrink a file so it fits the upload limit, turn photos of your homework into a PDF, and much more.

What can it do?

🔗 MergeJoin several PDFs into one file.
✂️ SplitBreak a PDF into pages or sections.
🗜️ CompressMake big files smaller for uploading or emailing.
🖼️ Images → PDFTurn photos of handwritten work into one PDF.
📝 Office → PDFConvert Word, PowerPoint and Excel files.
🔄 PDF → Images / WordGet pictures or editable text out of a PDF.
🔍 OCRMake scanned pages searchable and copyable.
↩️ Rotate & reorderFix sideways pages and put pages in order.
🔢 Page numbersAdd page numbers to your assignment.
💧 WatermarkAdd your name or a “Draft” stamp.
✍️ SignDraw or type a signature on a form.
🔒 PasswordLock a PDF, or unlock one you own.

Step-by-step guides

Turn photos of my homework into one PDF
  1. Take clear photos of each page in good light, with the page flat.
  2. Open Stirling PDF and search for “Image to PDF”.
  3. Select all your photos. Put them in the correct page order.
  4. Choose “Merge into single PDF”, then click Convert.
  5. Download the PDF and check every page before you hand it in.

Tip: if the file is too big to upload, follow the “Compress” guide next.

My file is too big to upload — compress it
  1. Search for “Compress”.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Pick a compression level. Start low; go higher only if it is still too big.
  4. Click Compress and download the new file.
  5. Open it and make sure the text and pictures are still easy to read.
Combine my notes or worksheets into one file
  1. Search for “Merge”.
  2. Upload all the PDFs you want to join.
  3. Drag them into the order you want (or sort by name).
  4. Click Merge and download the combined PDF.
Convert a Word or PowerPoint file to PDF
  1. Search for “Convert” and choose “File to PDF” (Office to PDF).
  2. Upload your .docx, .pptx or .xlsx file.
  3. Click Convert and download the PDF.

Tip: always check the PDF — fonts and layouts can shift slightly.

Fix sideways or upside-down pages
  1. Search for “Rotate” to turn every page, or “Multi Tool” to rotate single pages.
  2. Upload the PDF and click the rotate arrows until each page is upright.
  3. Save / download the fixed PDF.
Copy text from a scanned page (OCR)
  1. Search for “OCR”.
  2. Upload the scanned PDF and choose the language of the text.
  3. Click Run OCR, then download the result.
  4. You can now search the PDF and copy its text.

Tip: OCR works best on clear, printed text. Handwriting may not be recognised well.

Take only the pages I need (split / select pages)
  1. Search for “Split” or “Select pages”.
  2. Upload the PDF and type the page numbers, e.g. 1,3,5-8.
  3. Click the button to process, then download.

Stay safe

  • Only upload your own school work — never other people's private documents.
  • Always open and check your downloaded file before handing it in.
  • Keep a copy of your original file in case something goes wrong.
  • Using a tool to change a file does not change who did the work — hand in your own work.
